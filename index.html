SLIPPAGE-AWARE POSITION SIZING

The price you saw isn't the price you got.

Every retail calculator assumes you're filled at the price you decided on, the instant you decided. MarketSpike measures the two costs that assumption ignores — pipeline latency and expected slippage — and reports the size, and the exposure gap, that follow from what actually happened on the wire.
